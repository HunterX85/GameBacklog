Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account?
No — you can browse and track games without signing in. Creating an account lets your backlog sync and persist tied to that account.

How do I delete my account and data?
Open Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all backlog data associated with it. This action can't be undone.

How do I back up or move my backlog?
Settings → Export Backlog saves your games as a JSON file. Use Settings → Import Backlog to bring games from an exported file back into the app.

Where does the game data and cover art come from?
Game information and cover art are provided by IGDB.com, a Twitch company.
